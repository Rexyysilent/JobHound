# Current implementation changes

## October 23, 2026: preserve distinct posting identities

- Replace fuzzy cross-posting collapse in V3 and default V4.2 with explicit
  posting identity; preserve separate requisitions and applicant locations.
- Retain meaningful URL query/path differences and Unicode names, including
  Bengali text and distinct C++/C# specialisms. Deterministic selection no longer
  depends on input order or acquisition timestamps.
- Give V4.2 URL-bound IDs and preserve successful old notifications only when
  immutable receipt-linked evidence proves the same posting. Failed sends,
  conflicting identities and incomplete history do not establish that proof.
- Add synthetic pipeline, replay, storage and receipt regressions. Existing
  duplicate/conflict controls now supply the posting identity or accepted
  original-page lineage they depend on.

See [identity rules and migration boundaries](opportunity_identity.md), including
the V3 resurfacing and V5.5/outbox compatibility limits.

## Evidence and action correctness

- Exact 60-audio-minute compensation retains its output unit. Vendor work pace,
  approval processing and payment-delay claims remain separately attributed;
  bounded digest excerpts do not invent hourly earnings or first-payment dates.
- Optional dated equipment declarations keep webcam compatibility explicit.
  Phones are not presumed webcams; required, preferred and assessment-only
  demands retain separate action consequences and completed-work bookkeeping.
- Multi-posting JSON-LD pages require one unambiguous exact-URL role. Sibling
  degrees and campaign pay cannot supply the requested leaf's evidence.
  See [the behavior and configuration guide](handoff_boundaries.md).

- Explicit payout minima are compared with the exact fixed amount from the same
  compensation observation. Fee tiers are not minima, hypothetical aggregation
  is not a balance, and an allocated-work plan cannot bypass the amount check.
- Optional dated language declarations distinguish spoken/written capability
  and task preferences. Conflicts remain checks; profile evidence survives
  immutable context capture and is rebuilt when an assessment is reused.
- Reviewed outbound observations retain bounce and alternate-message history
  without establishing buyer replies, conversion or contact verification.
  Record-only events leave action material and delivery revision selection intact.

- Job status updates render compact lifecycle labels, reasons and safe next
  steps. Exhausted verification leads no longer appear as full Verify cards.
  Active cards, completed-step waiting advice and immutable delivery history
  retain their existing behavior; ranking and card limits are unchanged.

- Requirement-local skill/years/modality extraction; employer addresses and
  pronouns do not become applicant residency restrictions.
- Native JobPosting compensation, dates and applicant geography survive
  hydration. Exact identity preserves path/query case and rejects unrelated
  page blocks. Run metadata binds code, dependencies and frozen replay inputs.
- Literal approval is required for paid actions. Public suitability blockers
  veto readiness, including blockers added by the later optional risk review.
- Active and historical application attempts remain separate. Alias review
  binds exact observation identity and retains earlier scoped outcomes.

## Native buyers, exact roles and source budgets

- Bounded Discourse reads rederive buyer/seller ownership, quotations, demand
  dates, substantive updates and closure. Current buyer terms have revision IDs;
  older amounts remain history and do not establish an agreed quote.
- Exact Turing/micro1 readers retain native role identity and requirement
  sections; an empty shell or mismatched role stays partial.
- Visible application controls and navigation no longer supply native-role
  language or pay evidence. Dropdown options cannot reject an otherwise
  compatible role; genuine requirements outside forms remain enforced. Omitted
  sections have content hashes linked to the full fetched-body hash.
- The same boundary now covers V5.5 ATS HTML descriptions, Lever requirement
  lists and generic JobPosting/visible-page hydration. Original fields remain
  intact, generic form text cannot close a role, and uncertain control boundaries
  cannot claim complete extraction. Matching plain/HTML views use structural
  boundaries; unmatched plain claims are preserved. Legacy conversion is unchanged.
- Source-plan overlays share HTTP budgets with logical search/inspection caps.
  Cadence changes need adequately covered checks; partial or failed retrieval
  cannot count as a healthy zero-yield observation. Category/page failures
  preserve earlier successful records.

## Assessment, work and delivery review

- Assessment approvals belong to one exact step and revision. Work scope,
  allocation, routes, costs, prerequisites, deadlines and estimates retain
  their own identities and typed evidence.
- Agreement, delivery, acceptance, invoice, settlement and received payment
  are distinct events. Unknown effort or payout evidence stays unknown.
- Disposable handoff signals share the existing PriorityKey and receipt
  machinery. Supported urgency and substantive buyer changes can affect
  ordering; weak leads do not fill quotas.
- Accepted baselines are specific to each destination. Unsuitable historical
  drafts, mixed current drafts and attempted/uncertain sends have separate
  retirement, explicit reissue and reconciliation rules. Receipt history and
  immutable generations are retained.

## Local operation and compensation

- A keyless local workspace supports named profiles, diagnostics, reviewed
  inputs, fictional demos and closed-state backup/restore with checksums and
  database integrity checks. It rejects foreign paths, links and active state.
- Root public examples assert no qualifications. Fictional local demo policies
  are separate and selected explicitly. The demo clones its interpreter policy
  so a disabled live switch cannot produce incompatible outcome identities.
- Revenue, funding, valuations, pool-level payouts, customer values and expense
  allowances remain auditable claims and cannot become worker wages. Validated
  European number/unit formats retain literal ranges. Cash benefits retain
  conditions separately from base pay.

## Security and daily digest fixes

- SMTP and IMAP verify TLS certificates. Public-page connections use validated
  public IPs with original-host HTTP/TLS identity; unsupported proxies fail closed.
- JobPosting/parent identity and cache-boundary tests prevent unrelated native
  evidence from verifying another listing.
- Hosted-board copies remain excluded until an original is resolved. Resource
  pages and roundups fail vacancy gates in both headline and compact pools.
- Native buyer requests with over 60 days without substantive buyer activity
  become historical/watch records. Fresh buyer activity can restore eligibility;
  seller replies, acknowledgments and cosmetic edits cannot.
- Task fit and role priority are labeled separately rather than printed as a
  slash pair.

These are implemented regression protections. Independent recommendation quality,
expanded source effectiveness and live outcome integration remain the bounded
work listed in [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
