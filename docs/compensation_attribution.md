# Compensation attribution in V5.5

Money in a listing must belong to the offered work before it can supply selected
pay. Business revenue, funding, valuations, market sizes, assets, customer
contract values and deal sizes remain auditable
claims. Identified business expenses and development, equipment, laundry, meal or wellness
allowances also retain their original text and spans while being excluded from
wage selection and pay-conflict calculation.

The reader checks the subject immediately attached to each monetary expression.
An amount explicitly labelled salary, compensation, project budget or project
funding can retain its work scope even when nearby text mentions finance.
This preserves wages for finance specialists and buyer budgets for financial
automation work. Removing a business amount can expose another candidate, so
that candidate's attribution must also pass the same checks.

Separately labelled facts in one sentence can be segmented: “We raised USD 40M,
compensation: USD 20/hour.” Each resulting claim retains exact offsets into its
normalized native field and that field's SHA-256. A native forum claim also keeps
the buyer's post pointer and payload hash. An unlabelled alternative such as
“USD 5 per image or USD 60 per hour depending on the assignment” still abstains
from a single supported rate. Distinct explicit wage units remain a visible
conflict; funds and allowances cannot fabricate that conflict. Explicit per-worker
rates remain supported; a platform-wide payout statistic retains its group scope.

Source identity, original dates, requirements, account state and application
authority use their existing evidence paths. A retained monetary amount does
not establish allocated hours, net income, payment receipt or a delivery-time
estimate. Unsupported units and number formats remain unresolved. French salary
phrases and directly attached French units establish numeric interpretation;
currency or country alone cannot turn an English decimal into a thousands group.
Validated dot/space groups and decimal commas retain literal values and offsets.
Ordinary and typographic spaces support validated thousands groups outside
French phrases too. Malformed groups and truncated numeric continuations remain
unresolved; explicitly written age metadata after a complete range is separate
from its amounts. The saved French package now preserves
EUR 38,000-47,000 and its gross qualifier. Twice-yearly revaluation does not
establish an annual wage denominator. Only an explicitly stated period supplies
that period; neither salary nor bonuses become an estimated working-hour rate.

Housing, proximity, relocation, signing and sign-on cash claims retain their amounts,
qualifiers, conditions and any stated period under a separate benefit scope.
They appear on release cards separately from base pay and cannot provide wage
ranking or create a salary conflict. A benefit-only posting still has unknown
base pay. Longer conditions and additional claims remain in the uncapped audit;
cards explicitly mark shortened wording and omitted display rows. Native forum
benefits retain buyer actor, post pointer and payload hash, and follow the same
current/superseded/ambiguous term history rules as other native claims.
An explicitly labelled profit-share bonus also remains a separate claim with
its stated result year; it does not establish current entitlement. A dated
salary average remains a historical benchmark. A current salary can cite such a
benchmark without losing its own amount. Native list markers are parsed only
under the current pay or benefits section; negative claims and deductions stay
unresolved and exact source text remains unchanged.

Explicit million, billion, trillion and abbreviated business scales preserve
their numerical magnitude while business attribution keeps them out of worker
pay. Insurance-case valuation is attached to the cases, not to an employee's
wage; explicit insurance-related salary, commission and buyer budgets retain
their work scope. Capital, customer credit, asset transfers, advertising spend,
grantmaking and company/market size are financial context, rather than offered
wages. Technology, meal and credited-card allowances keep their native claims
without providing a wage. Identifying a forum buyer cannot resolve a truncated
financial subject or promote an unattributed amount to a project budget.
Unlabelled alternatives and unsupported aggregate claims still
require review rather than automatic ownership or a fabricated denominator.

The regression controls exercise native normalization, policy, digest, exact
claim provenance, independently labelled clauses and saved buyer posts. Frozen
reviews compare the complete assessments and decisions, including rank changes
and previously excluded claims; selected-pay summaries alone are insufficient.
