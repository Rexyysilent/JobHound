# Payout minimums and project amounts

A listed payment method does not establish that a particular project can be paid
through it. The V5.5 engine compares an exact fixed project amount with an explicit
minimum in the same compensation observation. For example, a fictional USD 18
project cannot satisfy a wire payout condition requiring an amount above USD 50.
Exactly USD 50 also fails that strict condition; an inclusive minimum differs.

The assessment retains `payout_feasibility`, including the route, literal source
span, observation identity, currency, comparison and result. Payout thresholds
remain source evidence rather than worker wages. Missing amounts, hourly rates,
unknown or mismatched currencies and ambiguous conditions stay unknown. Fee
waivers, tier thresholds and bonuses do not establish a payout minimum.
Cadence phrases such as "daily wise payment" do not name the Wise payment service.

An explicitly evidenced compatible alternate route can satisfy the amount check.
Another posting's terms, a payment logo, hypothetical future work and an assumed
balance cannot. There is no currency conversion or inferred aggregation.

An infeasible or unresolved amount check creates a verification step before work.
It also constrains an otherwise approved reviewed allocation, keeping its action
plan consistent. Closed records and completed-work invoice/payment bookkeeping
retain their existing actions. Compatibility alone establishes neither account
eligibility nor fees, settlement or money received.

Recognition is deliberately limited to supported English method/condition clauses.
This is not a complete parser of payment policies or a cross-document policy join.
Unrecognized wording needs review; no provider's current payout policy is bundled.
